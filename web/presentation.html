06Rating

One Elo — handicapped by the role you were dealt.

si  ←  si  +  k · ( woni − σ( si − dr − ρi ) )
woni
Per-seat outcome, not the team's.
dr
How hard the dealt role is to win.
ρi
Skill of the seats opposing you.

Reward over-performing a hard role against strong opponents.
